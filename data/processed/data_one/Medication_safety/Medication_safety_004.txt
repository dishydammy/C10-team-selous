Antibiotics can save lives, but they aren't always the answer.


Be Antibiotics Aware aims to improve antibiotic prescribing and use among consumers.


Using antibiotics appropriately helps to combat antimicrobial resistance.


CDC offers materials to help you learn about appropriate antibiotic use and antimicrobial resistance.


For professionals: Handouts, prescription pads, posters, and more.


HCPs and partner organizations can share these resource bundles during promotion efforts.


Key principles to guide efforts to improve antibiotic use, patient safety and outcomes: Leadership, Accountability, Expertise, Action, Tracking, Education, Reporting.
