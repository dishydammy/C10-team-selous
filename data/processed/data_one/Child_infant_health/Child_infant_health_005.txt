Wash caregiver and children hands, such as before preparing and eating food, and after using the toilet
 Use safe and clean water
Avoid going to the toilet in the open and disposing of human waste in the open
 Store food safely and use clean utensils
Keep the home and babyclothing as clean as possible to protect from infection
Store unsafe and toxic substances safely out of reach of children
Keep the outdoor areas around the home clean
Secure open waters such as pools, wells and ponds so children cannot access them Keep the home free of smoke from tobacco, cooking and heating
