- Almost all people affected by emergencies experience psychological distress.

- Emergencies disrupt mental health services.


- Emergencies worsen existing conditions and create new problems (family separation, substance use).
- International guidelines recommend MHPSS activities: community self-help, psychological first aid, clinical care.


Problems:
- Social: poverty, discrimination, family separation, loss of livelihoods.
- Mental health: depression, anxiety, PTSD, harmful substance use.
- Service access: damaged facilities, staff shortages, disrupted supply chains.


Recommended Actions:
- Include MHPSS in emergency preparedness.
- Strengthen community supports.
- Train frontline workers in psychological first aid.
- Provide evidence-based interventions.
- Protect rights of people with severe conditions.
