- Emergencies (Ebola, Zika, mpox, COVID-19) cause fear, stigma, grief, isolation, discrimination.
- CBRN emergencies add severe psychosocial consequences.


WHO Guidance:
- Support for health workers, caregivers, older adults, people in isolation/quarantine, and the general public.
- Publications: Psychological First Aid manuals, frameworks for radiological/nuclear emergencies, guides for Ebola outbreaks.
- Policy briefs: COVID-19 and mental health action.
- IASC guidance for multisectoral mental health and psychosocial support.


WHO Role:
- Coordinates global responses.
- Provides technical advice.
- Supports national authorities and partners.
