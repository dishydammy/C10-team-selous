- Over billion people live with a mental health condition.
- Effective strategies exist to promote, protect, and restore mental health.
- Mental health is integral to well-being and a basic human right.


- Mental health continuum: varies across individuals.
- Conditions include disorders, psychosocial disabilities, and states of distress.
- Treatment gaps remain wide globally.


Risks & Protective Factors:
- Risks: poverty, violence, inequality, substance use, harsh parenting, bullying.
- Protective: emotional skills, social interactions, education, decent work, safe communities.


Promotion & Prevention:
- Cross-sector collaboration needed.
- Suicide prevention strategies: limit access to means, responsible media reporting, adolescent social/emotional learning.
- Child/adolescent mental health promotion.
- Mental health at work.


Care & Treatment:
- Community-based care preferred over institutional care.
- Integrated services, psychosocial rehabilitation, peer support.
- Innovative approaches: non-specialist interventions, digital self-help tools.
