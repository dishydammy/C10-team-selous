- Nearly in people worldwide live with a mental disorder.
- Disorders disrupt daily life and cause long-term suffering if untreated.
- Effective prevention and treatment exist, but access is limited.


Types:
- Anxiety disorders (359 million people in 2021).
- Depression (280 million people in 2019).
- Bipolar disorder (37 million people in 2021).
- PTSD: develops after traumatic events.
- Schizophrenia: affects 23 million people worldwide.
- Eating disorders: 16 million people in 2021.
- Disruptive behavior/dissocial disorders: 41 million people.
- Neurodevelopmental disorders: intellectual disability, autism, ADHD.


Risk Factors:
- Poverty, violence, disability, inequality, genetics, emotional skills.


Health Systems:
- Treatment gaps are wide; only 29% of people with psychosis and one-third with depression receive care.
